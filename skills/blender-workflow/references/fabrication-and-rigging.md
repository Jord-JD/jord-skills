# Fabrication and rigging checks

Read the section matching the task. These checks supplement the normal scene and render inspection.

## Fabrication and 3D printing

Check units, overall dimensions, wall thicknesses, mating clearances, disconnected geometry, non-manifold/open edges, and normals. Verify that exported geometry retains the intended scale. Re-import or independently inspect the export where practical; a plausible render does not establish physical correctness.

## Rigging and animation

Inspect frame ranges, parent/bone relationships, constraints, and representative poses. Render key frames and inspect transitions or short sequences when motion matters. Check deformations, intersections, and whether the exported rig and animation behave in the target runtime.
